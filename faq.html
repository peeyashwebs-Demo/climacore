FAQ

Common questions.

What do SEER2, EER2, HSPF2 and AFUE mean?

They are efficiency ratings for cooling, heat pump heating and fuel-burning heating equipment. See our technology page.

How often should my system be serviced?

Follow your equipment manufacturer’s guidance. Many manufacturers recommend at least yearly service.

Do you offer free estimates?

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes, for urgent problems, with an emergency rate confirmed before dispatch (sample).

Do you offer financing?
