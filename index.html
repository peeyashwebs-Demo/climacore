Answers that make the next step easier

How much will it cost before you know the fault?

We charge a flat diagnostic fee, which is credited toward the repair if you go ahead. You will see the price before we start.

Can someone come out today if my system stops working?

We keep same-day slots open for urgent no-cooling and no-heating calls. Call us and we will give you a realistic arrival time.

Will I be pushed into replacing the whole system?

No. We explain what is wrong, what a repair costs, and what replacement would cost, then you decide.

What happens if the issue isn't fixed on the first visit?

We return to finish the job at no extra call-out charge when the same fault is still present.

Do you offer after-hours or emergency callouts?

Yes, for urgent problems. After-hours visits carry an emergency rate, which we confirm before dispatching.

How do payment and invoicing work?

You receive a written invoice on completion and can pay by card, bank transfer or finance, depending on the job.
