How much does it cost to fix an AC in Florida?

Anywhere from $150 to $1,500+, depending on what’s wrong. A simple capacitor or contactor can cost under $200. A failed compressor is a bigger conversation, and we’ll give you honest options either way.

How much does an air conditioner cost for a 2,000 sq ft home in Florida?

Expect roughly $7,500 to $10,000+ installed, depending on brand, efficiency rating and installation needs. We give real quotes, and financing is available.

What is the life expectancy of an air conditioner in Florida?

Typically 8–12 years. Florida humidity, salt air and near-constant use are hard on a system. If yours is over 10 years old and acting up, it may be time to upgrade.

Is it cheaper to repair or replace an AC unit?

It depends on age and damage. Use the $5,000 rule: if the repair cost multiplied by the system’s age is over $5,000, replacing usually makes more sense.

What’s the $5,000 rule for HVAC?

Multiply the repair cost by the age of the system. If the result is over $5,000, replacement is usually the better value. For example, a 12-year-old unit needing a $600+ repair is worth considering for replacement.

Why is my AC running but not cooling?

Common causes include low refrigerant, dirty coils or a failed part. If it blows air that isn’t cold, the system is working hard and failing, so call us before it gives up entirely.
