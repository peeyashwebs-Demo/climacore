How systems work, and what the numbers mean.

Efficiency ratings

SEER2 and EER2

Cooling efficiency ratings. Higher numbers generally mean less energy per unit of cooling.

HSPF2

Heating efficiency of heat pumps. Higher is more efficient.

AFUE

Fuel efficiency of furnaces and boilers, as a percentage.
